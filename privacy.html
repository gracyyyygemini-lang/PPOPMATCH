Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

PopMatch is a free marketplace for University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign students. Your privacy is important to us.

1. Information We Collect

We collect your UIUC email address for verification purposes to ensure a safe marketplace. Usage data and listing details are stored securely via Supabase.

2. How We Use Data

Data is used strictly to facilitate housing and subleasing matches. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Security

We use industry-standard encryption to protect your information.
